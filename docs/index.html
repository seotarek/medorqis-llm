Eliminating arithmetic hallucination in clinical AI.

Autoregressive language models predict tokens probabilistically. When tasked with medical equations—such as CKD-EPI eGFR or pediatric mg/kg dosage ceilings—they hallucinate numbers that appear plausible but violate physiological reality.

MedOrqis-LLM decouples clinical reasoning from computation: it processes narratives and dialects, and routes any risk score or dosage calculation to 12 deterministic, zero-token guideline algorithms.

System Design

The Dual-Track Architecture

Why MedOrqis-LLM was trained as a specialized 6B LoRA adapter rather than a monolithic, expensive black box.

Track 1: Narrative & Dialect Reasoning

Handled inside the 6B transformer weights via QLoRA SFT. The model excels at linguistic comprehension, clinical synthesis, and parsing unstructured dialectal terminology into standardized nomenclature.

  • ▸ Bilingual Arabic & English medical comprehension
  • ▸ Regional colloquial symptom translation (Egyptian, Gulf)
  • ▸ Protected Health Information (PHI) recognition & redaction
  • ▸ Emergency red-flag symptom triage (e.g. Anaphylaxis)
Track 2: Zero-Token Mathematical Engines

Mathematical calculations, multi-drug interaction graphs, and risk scoring are strictly prohibited inside the neural weights. The model emits a structured function call targeting validated Python calculators.

  • ▸ CKD-EPI 2021 Race-Free & Bedside Schwartz Pediatric eGFR
  • ▸ ESC 2024 CHA2DS2-VA (Sex-neutral Atrial Fibrillation stroke risk)
  • ▸ Cockcroft-Gault CrCl with automatic IBW/ABW weight gating
  • ▸ CURB-65 pneumonia mortality risk & Wells PE pre-test probability

Python Inference (PEFT) Transformers + Peft 
import torch
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer
from peft import PeftModel

base_id = "Applied-Innovation-Center/Karnak-6B-v1.0"
lora_id = "seotarek/medorqis-llm"

tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(base_id, trust_remote_code=True)
base_model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(base_id, load_in_4bit=True, device_map="auto")
model = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base_model, lora_id)

prompt = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(messages, tools=CLINICAL_TOOLS, tokenize=False)

Passing the clinical tools schema to apply_chat_template triggers valid ChatML tool calling representations.
